New to mining? Read this ↓

Laser mining (easiest to start): fit Mining Lasers (hardpoints), a Refinery, a Prospector Limpet Controller, a Collector Limpet Controller, and Cargo Racks + Limpets. Fly to a ring, fire a prospector limpet at an asteroid to see its content, shoot High-content rocks with the lasers, and let collector limpets auto-scoop the chunks into your refinery.

Core mining (bigger payouts, e.g. Void Opals): also fit a Pulse Wave Analyser (utility) to spot glowing asteroids, a Seismic Charge Launcher, and an Abrasion Blaster. Find a motherlode asteroid, shoot the surface deposits, drop seismic charges in the blue fissures to build the ring to optimal, then detonate to crack it and collect the gems.

Find a hotspot: honk + DSS-map a ringed planet to reveal hotspot rings for specific ores. Sell mining commodities at high-demand stations — use the “find best sell price” button when your hold fills up.
